## DECISIONS TAKEN

- The vanity's EQUAL split is the KIT's own (`equalDrawers` in `drawerSplitFor`), not a birth answer: the LISP's
  D8 makes the vanity's fronts equal by definition, a bare `computeCabinet('VANITY3')` must be the LISP, and a new kit
  has no golden to hold. The chest's (D20) stays B5's birth answer because CHEST3's golden is 4:3:2.
- The trap's U is cut AFTER the box's Cabineo pass (`applyVanityTrap`, beside the canopy's), not inside the
  per-column emission loop: T79 F14 joins a box by the tag of its WHOLE back wall and places its pockets and dia 15
  drills after the emission; splitting the wall in the loop would drop the top box's Cabineo silently. The pass
  moves each pocket (its `pts` and its `export` frame) and each drill with the board it lands in.
- Check #39 is YELLOW (the owner's "zostawimy informacje"); its message is the LISP's sentence without the "#39 "
  prefix (the row carries the number).
- `ownDepth: true` and `heightGroup: null`: the project's 558 and 770 must not replace the vanity's 500 and 600.
- Parts RAIL-BOTTOM / RAIL-TOP, role 'back' (a shell board), material 'board', registry carcase_horizontal.
- `sinks: 1` at birth: the LISP's worked example and its command's default.
- Floating is B4's (F13): declared in the defaults, never read by `computeCabinet`.

  `dowelVerdict` refuse a vanity in words (JOINTS_REFUSED, Check #31) and it keeps the puzzle. Teaching the three
  joint modules the rails is a change to engine/cabineo.js, biscuitJoints.js and dowels.js beyond this slice. The
  box's own Cabineo (T79 F14) works and splits with the wall.
- The U in the ROOM (3D): `3d/panelSolid.js` extrudes a rectangle plus socket notches, not an arbitrary outline;
  3d/* is D1's and A5's this turn. The U is on the CNC, the cut list and the boards' records.
- A vanity under a slope: the rails are not trimmed by the slope line.
