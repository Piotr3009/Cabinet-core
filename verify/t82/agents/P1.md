3. A notice the source writes with an em dash (`Width limited to 1100 mm ... `) is shown as `[U+2014]` in the table: the laws keep the dash out of what P1 writes, and the measured text is otherwise verbatim.
4. F17's corner chimney is the chat-fix stage with the chimney at x 0 (CLAUDE.md names no corner stage); the attach clamps that panel to x 10, which the table says.
