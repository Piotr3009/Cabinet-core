## DECISIONS TAKEN

- `z` means the distance of the back face from the wall line, which is what
  the eye and the `fp-wall` figure read. The store writes it as
  `inset_back_mm` = z less the clearance.
- Shift is read once, at the press (the `startDepthDrag` pattern, where Alt
  is read at the press). A Shift drag reports `moved: false` on its drop, so
  it never takes the x snap proposal.
- The y drag waits until the hand has travelled 2 px (T74 F13's own
  threshold), so a click lifts nothing. y and z snap to the hand's snap step,
  as x does, else to `editor.mmStep` 0.5.
- The y and z clamps also live in `updateUnitParams`, for free panels only,
  through the one `freePanelReach`. So the T74 `fp-floor` road (pinned to
  `updateUnitParams({ mount_height })`) and the right panel's Mount height
  field are clamped too, with the sentence. Cabinets are untouched.
- `moveFreePanelZ` writes through `updateUnitParams`, never through
  `setUnitInsets`. Its 300 cap, and its depth re-clamp, would shorten a panel
  standing across the wall.
- No obstacle stop on y or z: F16's clamps are the room's only. A panel
  pushed back into a chimney is said in words by `validateUnit` (D2's F17
  `obstaclesBeyond` there), not held silently.
- Where no far wall is found (a ray into the open), z has no upper limit.
