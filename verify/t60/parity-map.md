# T60 F5 · THE PARITY MAP — WHAT PRO STILL HAS THAT PBI DOES NOT

The owner: *"wszystkie funkcje muszą się pojawić w retail te co w PRO, tylko
inaczej poustawiane i inaczej poukładane — bardziej wizualnie."*

That is more than one night. PRO carries **62 modals and panels**, one of them
1546 lines and another 1141. So this turn ALSO produces the map that makes the
remaining turns cheap: every surface read from the code, with its purpose in one
line, whether a CLIENT needs it, where it landed in PBI today, and the engine and
store functions it drives.

**No row is a guess.** Each was produced by reading the file itself and grepping
`src/retail/**` for where its decisions live now. `lines` is the real line count.
