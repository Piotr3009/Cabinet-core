THE FIX (`src/lib/drawingExport.js`): `turnedTextOrigin(doc, s, x, y, angle,
align)` turns the anchor itself. It goes back along the text's own line by the
alignment's share of jsPDF's `getTextWidth`, and across it by jsPDF's own
'middle' offset (half the size, less the line-height descent). jsPDF is then
handed that baseline origin with the angle and no alignment. A flat text
never takes this road.

THE SVG: not changed. Chromium inks the rotated figure centred on its anchor
(table above): `text-anchor="middle"` and `dominant-baseline="central"` are
laid out before the `rotate()` transform, so they hold at any angle. E2's 0.9
mm did not reproduce in the browser the owner previews in. A renderer that
does not read `dominant-baseline` would set flat figures off by the same
amount. Changing the SVG would move every text of every sheet and the unit
card's pinned SVG for no gain on the glass.

PINS MOVED: none. The unit card's pinned SVG (`test/fixtures/t43-unit-card-
before.svg`) and every SVG are byte for byte as before. The flat page stream
