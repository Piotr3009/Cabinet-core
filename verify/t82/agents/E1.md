## What was built

- **The sheet** (`src/engine/drawings/setSheet.js`): `titleStrip` reads
  `company.logo` through `logoOf` and, when there is one, draws ONE `image`
  entity on `SHEET_MUTED` (`meta: 'logo'`, as the box was) fitted INTO the T71
  24 x 24 box with the picture's own ratio and centred (`fitLogo`: a 200 x 100
  PNG stands 24 x 12, 6 up from the box's foot). With a logo, the dashed
  placeholder and its two words ("LOGO", "(Settings)") give way; with none,
  the box is byte for byte as T71 drew it. New pure helpers, exported:
  `LOGO_TYPES`, `dataUrlType`, `imageSizeOf` (the size off the picture's own
  header: PNG IHDR, JPEG frame marker, SVG width / height or viewBox),
  `logoOf`, `fitLogo`. Only a `data:` URL of a PNG, JPG or SVG is a logo; a web
  address or a stray word leaves the box (the paper never fetches anything).
- **The PDF** (`src/lib/drawingExport.js`): the image branch of `drawSheet`
  hands jsPDF `pdfImageOf(e)`: the entity's `raster` when it has one, else the
  picture itself, and never an SVG (jsPDF 2.5.2 reads PNG and JPEG, no SVG);
  an SVG with no raster is skipped with a console warning and the page still
  binds. The visualisation's render is handed exactly as before.
- **How an SVG reaches the PDF**: rasterised ONCE, in the browser, when it is
  chosen: `readLogoFile` loads the SVG data URL into an `Image`, draws it on a
  canvas at `profile.drawings.set.logo.maxPx` (400) on its long side, ratio
  kept, and keeps `canvas.toDataURL('image/png')` beside it as `raster`. The
  SVG preview and the SVG file draw the vector (`<image href="data:image/svg+xml...">`);
  the PDF embeds the PNG, synchronously, in the same pass as every entity (no
  async export path was needed). A browser that taints the canvas is refused
  in words ("save it as a PNG and choose that").
- **Choosing the file** (`readLogoFile(file, profile, env)` in
  `drawingExport.js`): PNG, JPG or SVG by the file's type or, when the system
  gave none, by its extension; anything else refused in words ("A logo is a
  PNG, JPG or SVG picture; X is not one."). A PNG or JPG larger than `maxPx`
  on its long side is drawn down to it (PNG stays PNG with its transparency,
  JPG at 0.92); the kept data URLs may be at most `maxKb` (256) or the window
  says so and keeps nothing. The browser's hands (FileReader, Image, canvas)
  are a default argument, so the node test hands its own.
- **The window** (`src/components/DrawingModal.jsx`, PRO, EXEMPT below): in
  the Title block grid, right after Company, a cell "Logo (PNG, JPG or SVG)"
  with a 7 x 7 preview of the chosen logo, "Choose image" (a label over a
  `sr-only` file input, `accept="image/png,image/jpeg,image/svg+xml,.png,.jpg,.jpeg,.svg"`)
  and "Remove" (disabled with no logo). The summary reads "Title block:
  company, logo, drawn by, status, revision". Errors go to `notify` through
  `drawingErrorText` and to the console (T42-F0's law).
- **Storage**: the logo is kept WITH the company,
  `setTitleBlock({ company: { logo } })`, so the store's existing
  `rememberTitleBlockCompany` writes it under the same key as the company name
  (`cabinet-core.titleBlock.company`) and `recallTitleBlockCompany` gives the

## DECISIONS TAKEN

1. **Storage key**: no new key. The logo lives at `company.logo` inside the
   company the store already remembers under `cabinet-core.titleBlock.company`
   (CLAUDE.md: *"stored with the title block's company in the same place the
   company name is remembered on this computer"*); it rides the job too, as
   the company does.
2. **Size cap**: at most 400 px on the long side (24 mm at 400 px is past 400
   dpi) and at most 256 KB of data URLs kept (`profile.drawings.set.logo`,
   PROPOSED). The reason for the cap: the logo is remembered in localStorage
   AND rides every job saved on this computer's shelf, which shares the same
   few megabytes; past the cap the window refuses in words and keeps nothing.
3. **SVG to the PDF**: rasterised once at choose time through a canvas (PNG,
   400 px long side), kept as `raster` beside the SVG; the sheet and the SVG
   file draw the vector, the PDF the PNG. No async export path.
4. **With a logo the dashed placeholder goes**: the box's dashes and its two
   words are a placeholder; a client-facing logo is drawn on white without a
   dashed frame round it. No logo: the box exactly as today.
5. **The placeholder's word stays "(Settings)"** although the logo is chosen
   in the Drawings window: CLAUDE.md says "no logo = the box as today", and the
   frozen look of T71's strip is not this F's to reword. The owner may ask.
6. **Only data URLs of PNG, JPG and SVG are logos**: the paper never fetches a
   web address (the preview and the PDF would disagree, and the PDF cannot).
7. **The DXF** draws the frame the picture fills (T71's rule for the render):
   R12 has no picture entity; the DXF is AutoCAD only.
