## DECISIONS TAKEN

- A unit's front line is its door face, the house's sum (`frontFaceDepthOf`,
  `cornerFaceMm`), measured from the unit's own back line (`backStandoff`), so
  a cabinet stood off for a pipe counts.
- `freeBesideUnit` takes the same filtered obstacles (a fourth unfiltered
  site; `setUnitInsets` settles left and right insets through it). Placement
  (`addUnit`, `moveUnitToWall`, `cornerLSlots`) is untouched: a panel is placed
  at inset 0, where the filter changes nothing.
- A free panel is cut at its own `board_t`; a caliper-measured carcass slot no
  longer reaches a free panel (it is a board of its own, typed by hand), so the
  room's box, the cut list and the field agree.
- A capped back inset of a free panel is said in words (the room refuses in
  words); a cabinet's cap at 300 stays silent as it was.
- The refusal sentence takes the type's label up to a comma or a spaced dash,
  its capital lowered unless it is an acronym ("D/W panel").
- `attachPlan` refuses past the far wall for every surface (laying a panel
  flat makes its box deeper), not only for the front faces.
