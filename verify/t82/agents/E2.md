## 2. What changed, file by file

`src/engine/drawings/wallElevation.js` (mine):
- T41 F5c's drop rule is retired (its block replaced by the T82 F21 block,
  the owner's words, the probe's rows).
- `isFaceFront(p)`: a FRONT or DRAWER-FRONT that is not an inner front behind
  a door (`meta.inner`, T78 F15), one predicate for every front chain.
- `frontStacks(members)` (new export): every unit's own face fronts, bottom
  and top edges up the wall, FRONT, GAP, FRONT; units with the same stack
  (half a millimetre) share one. Two units' edges are never pooled.
- `frontChainLines(members)` (new export): the stacks on the LINES of the
  front chain; the richest stack (most fronts, then nearest the chain) takes
  the inner line, a stack shares a line with every stack it does not overlap
  in height (the wall run over the base run) and steps out a line where it
  does.
- `leafChains(members, { ends = true })` (new export): LEAF, GAP, LEAF, every
  face front's two x edges, with the run's two carcass ends (the floor run:
  the chain adds up to the unit chain) or without them (a hung run: first
  leaf to last).
- `heightChains(members)`: publishes `stacks`, `fronts` (every stack's
  figures), `bandEdges`, `bands`; the `floorMm` option is read no more (a
  caller passing it changes nothing). `frontEdges` (the pooled list) is gone.
- `verticalChains` (the T40 composer, on no sheet of the set): draws the
  stacks on their lines, the bands one line further out.

`src/engine/drawings/setElevation.js` (INT's file; my hunks are its import
line and `runChains` only, so E3's `projectedRun` hunks do not meet mine):
- RIGHT: every stack of `frontChainLines` as its own `chainV`, line k at
  `chainFirst + k * (chainSecond - chainFirst)`; the inner line carries the
  extension lines, as T71's inner chains do.
- BOTTOM: on the fronts and X-ray sheets the floor run's leaf chain on the
  second line (`-chainSecond`) under its unit chain, the overall stepped out
  to `-chainThird` (only when the leaf chain is drawn; the no-fronts sheet
  keeps T71's two lines exactly).
- A HUNG run's leaves (every band over the floor, one chain per run of
  touching units by `widthGroups`, first leaf to last) under its wall units at
  `base - chainFirst`, in the open wall; it needs the room of two chain lines
  there (`chainSecond`) and is not hung where a unit or a worktop stands in
  that room.
- The leaf chains are pushed LAST, so every chain index T71's tests read is
  unchanged.

`src/engine/drawings/setChains.js`: untouched. The leader is T71's lift.

## 4. How the leader is drawn

T71's grammar, unchanged: a figure that does not fit its segment stands 4.0
or 7.2 paper mm beside the chain on the side away from the object, on a FINE
leader from the dimension line at the segment's middle; consecutive lifted
figures alternate the two levels. Up the right the leader is horizontal and
the figure reads up the sheet; on a leaf chain the leader is vertical.

## 8. DECISIONS TAKEN

- A front chain is ONE UNIT'S STACK (probe row 11): identical stacks share a
  chain, stacks that do not overlap in height share a line, overlapping ones
  step out a line (the walk's kitchen: two lines; the proof kitchen: three,
  inside the band). A wall with more than three overlapping stacks in one
  band puts its fourth line past the band's 26 mm; the walk's and the proof
  kitchen's sheets are tested inside the box.
- The floor run's leaf chain spans the run carcass end to carcass end (the
  owner's DXF bottom chain, `40 · 600 · 3 · 600 · 40`), so it adds up to the
  unit chain; the figures between two units' leaves (in-frame 44, lay-on 3)
  are printed. It draws no extension lines (as T71's overall): the unit
  chain's figures stand over the pair's gap.
- THE WALL RUN'S PAIR, as the orchestrator asked to try: ABOVE its unit chain
  does not read (on the walk's kitchen at 1:15 the second line over the WUD
  is 2475 and its figures stand on the ceiling line at 2500; on the proof
  kitchen the line passes over the ceiling and through "CEILING 2400").
  UNDER the wall units, in the open wall over the worktop, it reads cleanly on
  both kitchens, and is what is built: one chain per run of touching units,
  first leaf to last (with its carcass ends the proof kitchen's last 1.5 hung
  over the fridge beside the run), not hung where a unit or worktop stands in
  the room it needs. T71's band test is amended to that law.
- The face fronts only: an inner drawer front behind a door (PANTRY,
  BUD_INT) is in no chain of the fronts or X-ray sheet; it is on the cut list.
- The gap figure prints as `formatMm` prints every figure (3, 376.5), not
  the DXF's two decimals.
