## WHAT IS CUT

- **The two keys.** `params.drawer_split: 'equal'` and `params.columns: 1 | 2`.
  The engine reads them only on a kit whose TYPE carries `drawerColumns: true`:
  WARDROBE_CHEST2 / 3 / 4 tonight, and B2's VANITY2 / 3 / 4.
- **Equal.** `drawer_split: 'equal'` gives n ones, exact: each front is
  fix(available / n + 0.5) and the top one takes the rounding (LISP
  `vanity-front-heights`). CHEST3 at 647 cuts 213 / 213 / 212. CHEST2 and
  CHEST4 are byte for byte what they were.
- **Born equal.** `projectStore.newUnit` writes `drawer_split: 'equal'` and
  `columns: 1` on a `drawerColumns` kit. This is T48 F3's law of the plinth: the
  answer is written where a unit is born, never in `defaultParamsFor`.
  - Every road that places a chest goes through `addUnit -> newUnit`: the
    library, retail's ADD CHEST OF DRAWERS, and add beside a wardrobe.
  - A loaded project never goes through `newUnit`. A chest saved before tonight
    opens at the split it was cut to.
- **Columns 2** (KIT_VANITY.lsp 5(c2), D10 to D12):
  - **The DIVIDER.** A VPART (the house's vertical partition) with id
    `DIVIDER`, cut in the carcass board.
    - Position: x (W - G) / 2, on the BOTTOM (y G), under the TOP (H - G), from
      z G, D - G deep. That is 18 x 611 x 550 at x 391.
    - Its runner rows are on both faces. The left face is drilled as BUL is; the
      right face, the same part turned over, as BUR is (T21 F12.2's mirror rule).
      Both use the sides' rows.
    - Being a VPART, it takes the house's head and foot divider joint (CHAT FIX
      17.10.2026, `dividerJointCuts`, receivers BOTTOM and TOP) and the BACK's
      partition back screws, both through the roads every divider already takes.
    - `meta.locked`: it belongs to the columns.
  - **Each column's stack.**
    - Openings: G .. (W - G) / 2 and (W + G) / 2 .. W - G, 373 each.
    - Boxes: the opening less 10, at the opening + 5 (363 at x 23 and 414). The
      walls are the box less 2 GB (327); the bottom is 13 longer (340).
    - Tags: Z1D{i} / Z2D{i}, the wardrobe's column tags.
    - Fronts: (W - 6) / 2 = 397 at x 1.5 and 401.5, ids `{unit}-Z{c}D{i}-F` (the
      wardrobe's column face id).
    - Each front screw keeps its distance from its OWN box side: column 1 is at
      89.5 / 316.5, column 2 at 80.5 / 307.5.
  - **BOM.** A runner pair per box, the second column's counted after the
    first's. A rod per box, with Blum's threshold asked on the COLUMN's opening
    (373) and box.
  - **Room.** `hardware3d` places each column's pairs and rods on that column's
    own boxes.
  - **Derived record.** `columns: 2`, `drawer_front_x`, `column_openings` and
    `divider_x`. The box and front figures are a column's.
    `assemblies.drawerFronts` stays one row per height.
  - **Refusals, in words.** A box with no room for its walls gives
    DRAWER_COLUMNS_REFUSED and one column is cut. A double drawer row on two
    columns gives DOUBLE_DRAWER_REFUSED and one box is cut in each column.
- **What the chest does not take.** It keeps its BACK. It takes no rails, no
  void (its wall_gap is the BUDR's) and no basin or U (`sinks: 1` changes
  nothing).

## ONE LAW ONE PATH (for B2)

B2 reuses these in `src/engine/cabinet.js`, keyed on `type.drawerColumns` and the
params, never on a kit's id:

- `drawerColumnsOf(type, params)` returns 1 or 2.
- `drawerSplitOf(type, profile, params)` is read in `normalizeParams`.
- `drawerColumnsPlan({ W, G, GB, unitNum, profile })` gives the per-column
  opening, box, front, tag, front id, screw shift and walls.
- `drawerColumnsDivider({ W, H, D, G })` gives the divider.

In `computeCabinet` the drawer block carries `budr.cols` and `budr.divider`. The
box, bottom and front emission loops run `for (const c of budr.cols)`; the
divider's runner drilling and the per-column BOM sit in guarded blocks on
`budr.divider`. A `drawerStyle: 'budr'` kit with `drawerColumns: true` goes
through all of it unchanged. The vanity's U belongs in the BB emission of the box
loop, and its rails replace the BACK: the divider's back screws only go in where
a BACK part exists.

## DECISIONS TAKEN

- **Equal is a birth answer, not an engine default.** `newUnit` writes `drawer_split: 'equal'` because CHEST3's golden is 4:3:2 (FROZEN 1; KIT_VANITY.lsp D20). An old project's CHEST3 keeps 4:3:2 until the owner presses Equal (the panel button proposed above).
- **The rules are read only on a `drawerColumns` kit.** `columns` and `drawer_split` are ignored on a kitchen BUDR (nothing invented).
- **Equal is exact:** the top front takes the rounding (LISP D8). This also clears CHEST3's DRAWER_FRONTS_OVER at 647.
- **The DIVIDER is `part: 'VPART'`, `id: 'DIVIDER'`.** The LISP names it DIVIDER, and it is the house's vertical partition: the room, the CNC frame, the sticker (`partDisplayName`, "DIVIDER") and the divider joint all follow. Its runner holes on the two faces stand back to back, as the wardrobe's divider between two drawer columns has them (T32 F4, T79 F3).
- **Front ids on two columns:** `{unit}-Z{c}D{i}-F`, the wardrobe's column face ids. B1 left the front ids to the engine slice.
- **Front screws on two columns (DERIVED from turn 24 F3.2):** each screw keeps the one-column distance from its own box side.
- **Check numbers:** none new in this slice. The refusals are engine warnings in words: DRAWER_COLUMNS_REFUSED is new, DOUBLE_DRAWER_REFUSED is the house's own.
