## DECISIONS TAKEN
1. "None" is read from what Settings already writes: `runners.variant 'T'`
   on the design itself (push-to-open; never the profile's TIP-ON fallback),
   or `hardware.handles 'none'` (T32). No new field and no new writer.
   - For the owner: a fresh project's BOM falls back to TIP-ON runners
     (`movento.defaultVariant 'T'`), yet its sheets show knobs, because a
     fallback is not a choice.
2. The default draws on the wall fronts and X-ray sheets only. It does not
   draw on the 3D, the plans, the sections or the unit card. A knob on the
   workshop card where nothing is drilled would mislead the bench.
3. A front whose door or unit says push (`meta.push`, `params.pocket.push`),
   or whose role is not `front`, takes no default (the HAWA pocket door).
4. A default bar that does not fit is drawn at its reference point, as
   `handleMarks` already draws a chosen bar that does not fit.
5. `PLINTH_HIDDEN` colour `#828282` (ACI 253), PROPOSED. Legend label
   "Plinth, below the cut", PROPOSED.
6. The face toward the room is the side where the unit's own fronts stand.
   With no fronts: +z for a board along the run; for a board across it, the
   side where its role's other board runs (the true L), else the unit's
   middle.
7. CLAUDE.md's "a rail": no rail is hidden under a plan's cut today. The
   predicate in `setPlan.js` is where one joins.
