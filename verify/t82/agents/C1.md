## WHAT IT DOES

- FL carries `fluteProfile: 'groove' | 'reed'` and, for a reed, `reedDiameterMm`.
  A reed decoration cuts a row of CONVEX half-rounds TOUCHING (no flat), the
  crests on the face, the valleys `reedDiameterMm / 2` into the board, sharp.
  The side margins are FL's `grooveInsetMm`; top and bottom the reeds run to the
  edges. A field the diameter does not divide is centred and the remainder is
  said in the Check (never a stretched reed).
- One CNC operation per VALLEY: kind `reed`, tool `{ kind: 'reedProfile',
  diameter }`, layer `FRONT_REED_D<depth>_S<start>_T<diameter>` (the FLUTE
  grammar, `operationLayer`), the centre line as the vector, the section it
  leaves in `crossSection` (`edge: 'start' | 'end'` on the two valleys that lie
  on the field's edges: one flank and a vertical wall to the flat margin).
- Check #46 (yellow): "Reeds leave N mm under the valley" when under
  `profile.front.flute.reedNoteUnderValleyMm` (10), and the remainder sentence.
- A NEW Fluted choice is born reeds (`newFrontDecoration`, read by
  `frontStyleSelection` and the Activate button). Old FL fronts stay grooves.

## DECISIONS TAKEN

1. **Check number**: #46 (the brief, section 6), CLAUDE.md's sentence kept:
   "Reeds leave N mm under the valley". The remainder sentence rides the same
   row (one row for the reeds), yellow as #32: the leaf is cut, a person decides.
2. **Old projects and the T76 goldens**: the FL recipe in the profile is T76's,
   untouched, and `defaultFrontDecoration('FL')` (the record the classifier
   re-makes, and T76 F4's test) is the grooves. A saved FL record with no
   `fluteProfile` normalises to exactly what it did (no key added) and is
   grooves. The default `reed` lives in `profile.front.flute.profile` and is
   applied only where a NEW choice is born: `newFrontDecoration`, called by
   `frontStyleSelection` (every road that picks a style: Settings, the right
   panel, the wizard, retail's adapter) and the Activate button. A reselected
   style keeps its own record, so an old FL project stays grooves until the
   owner chooses Reeds in the select.
3. **The arithmetic of the 596 front**: CLAUDE.md's "(596 - 100) / 20 = 24
   reeds exactly" is 24.8: 24 whole reeds, 16 mm over, centred (58 a side),
   said in the Check. The test holds that, and holds a 580 front (480 / 20 = 24
   exactly, margins 50, nothing said) as the case that divides.
4. **The field's edges**: the first and last valley lie on the margin line and
   meet the flat margin in a vertical wall as deep as the valley (the mockup's
   left edge; its right-hand slope reads as the cutter's run-out, not a
   choice). The manifest says it in words for CAM. PROPOSED with the margins.
5. **Layer**: `FRONT_REED_D10_S0_T20`, the FLUTE grammar with its own kind
   token, so a ballnose flute and a reed profile of the same numbers never
   share a layer (`frontLayerFindings`: one layer, one recipe). Every valley of
   a recipe, edges included, names the cutter's width (the diameter) in its
   section, so the layer agrees with itself.
6. **The profile path**: CLAUDE.md writes `profile.frontDecoration.FL`; the
   house path is `profile.front.decoration`. The reed numbers sit beside it in
   `profile.front.flute` (T76 F4's test pins the keys of `front.decoration`).
7. **The UI**: for a reed the ballnose's own numbers (cutter, depth, pitch)
   are hidden, the margin reads "Reed side margin"; the ladder is the
   profile's (`reedChoices`), never typed in the component.
8. **Retail's board rule** (`boardRefusal`, T76 F7): FL is asked what a pick
   gives (reeds of 20 need 13), since that is what a client gets.
9. **The mockup's labels**: the owner's "wariant b czyli na styk" is read by
   his own gloss, touching (the mockup labels touching "CHMURKA A"), as
   CLAUDE.md writes it; the flat between reeds is NOT this turn.

## AFTER THE REVIEW (25.10.2026, base fa3bf30d, branch t82s/C1-fix)

The adversarial review of PARTS C, D, E (`/home/user/t82/review-CDE.json`)
found four faults in the reeds; three are this slice's, each fixed in its own
commit with a test that reproduced it first (`test/turn82-f15-reed.test.js`,
the tests named "after the review (a)", "(b)", "(c)").

- (a) reeds:0, the screw under a crest. The back-bore rule read each valley's
  strip as a flat pocket as deep as the valley, so every reeded drawer front
  of BUDR2 / BUDR4 on 18 and 22 was refused (no reeds in the room, red #9,
  held from the CNC). `reedDepthOverDisc` measures the reed's own depth over
  the bore's disc; a screw under a crest passes (10.7 mm left on 22), a screw
  under a valley is still refused in words with the depth and the board left
  (a 603 BUDR2, its 600 front's screws 0.5 mm off the valleys).
- (b) reeds:2, the J-pull. A TOP J with reeds was refused with "Increase its
  frame or margin", which can never help. The refusal stays (a 10 mm valley
  through the J's 4.2 mm lip is real); the sentence names what works:
  Grooves in the Flute profile, or another handle (on an edge the reeds run
  to), or the Reed side margin past the J's reach (on a margin edge). Each
  remedy is tested to work. The back-bore sentence says a bore wider than a
  reed always meets a valley (no "another width" for a cup).
- (c) reeds:3, the field's edges. The two edge valleys (one flank and a wall)
  are their own layer `FRONT_REEDEDGE_D10_S0_T20`, their true width (r), their
  own vector meaning and layer label; `frontLayerFindings` reads the edge, so
  one layer is never both sections. The manifest's reed note says it for CAM.
  `verify/t82/goldens/golden-fl-reed.json` (new this turn) re-written: the two
  edge layers and the FRONT DXF fingerprint, nothing else.
- reeds:1 (the room solid with a side J-pull) is D1's file; D1 fixes it.

DECISIONS TAKEN in the fix round:
- (b) is a refusal with honest remedies, not reeds stopped short of the J:
  stopping them changes the run-to-the-edges rule (PROPOSED), the owner's to

- (c) the two edges of a field are one recipe, mirrored (the wall on the line,
