## The one sign, and the choice it leaves

THE MIRROR IS GLOBAL, ONE SIGN, on every wall alike (rows 4 to 8: cross = -1 on A, B, C and D). Its root is the projector's plan frame (src/engine/drawings/projector.js:422): screen (x, z) with Y up is the view from BELOW (row 3), while the front and the section views are true (rows 1, 2). The plan sheet carries it whole: `throughWall` puts the projector's (x, z) through the wall's frame `at` (src/engine/drawings/setPlan.js:36) into the room's plan (x, y), whose y is the world's Z (src/engine/room.js:187), and `layoutSetSheet` lays that y UP the paper (src/engine/drawings/setSheet.js:190). The key plan does the same on its own (src/engine/drawings/setSheet.js:479, its comment src/engine/drawings/setSheet.js:478: "Y up, with wall A at the bottom"). The unit card already mends the same projector view by flipping y (src/engine/drawings/unitCard.js:174, row 14).

Per wall (rows 5 to 8), both readings of the one mirror: walls A and C (along x on the paper) read with x along the wall reversed, or with the depth axis y reversed; walls B and D (along y) read with y along the wall reversed, or with the depth axis x reversed. ONE flip mends all four, and there are exactly two, never both:

1. **flip y** (room y down the paper, as the unit card and the room editor's own top view draw it, src/components/RoomModal.jsx:269): wall A at the TOP, C at the bottom, D on the left, B on the right; wall A's numbers 01, 02, DR01 left to right as on its fronts sheet, wall C's reversed. This is the reading CLAUDE.md's own test asks for ("the unit numbers' x on the plan increase left to right as on the fronts sheet for wall A; for wall C ... they decrease") and its sentence "shows them in the SAME order".
2. **flip x**: wall A at the bottom, C at the top, but B on the LEFT and D on the right; wall A's numbers DR01, 02, 01 left to right (reversed against the fronts sheet).

CLAUDE.md's "wall C at the top, wall A at the bottom, wall D on the left, wall B on the right" is exactly what B0 draws (row 9), the view from below: no true plan has all four. The probe's reading: flip y (1), which keeps D left, B right and the fronts' order of wall A, and puts wall A at the top; it is said in the PR as a DECISION against the letter of "wall A at the bottom". BEWARE the test CLAUDE.md names: it reads x only and PASSES on B0 (the x order is the same before and after a y flip); the mirror is caught by y or by the turn (A, B, C, D clockwise on the paper, row 9).

The lines that carry the sign for E1 (the plan's frame, B0): src/engine/drawings/setPlan.js:36 `at` (every entity of the plan but the chains); the chains, which read the walls' y and inward y directly: src/engine/drawings/setPlan.js:323 and src/engine/drawings/setPlan.js:326 (and the line between); the extent src/engine/drawings/setPlan.js:63 (`measurePlan`, for the centring); the marker's arrowhead src/engine/drawings/setPlan.js:263 (it reads along and inward directly); and the key plan's `P` at src/engine/drawings/setSheet.js:479. `place` (src/engine/drawings/setSheet.js:190) is shared by every sheet and must not move. Independently of the mirror, the marker's arrows point to the wall's START while the section looks to its END (rows 10 to 13, src/engine/drawings/setPlan.js:263 against src/engine/drawings/setSection.js:116): the tip's sign there is a second, separate finding for E1.
