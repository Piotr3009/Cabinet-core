## DECISIONS TAKEN

- **Plinth height "as today".** It is the project's toe kick on the unit (`leg_height`), shown in
  Construction; there is no new field. The LISP's `vanityPlinth` 100 equals `baseUnit.legHeight`
  100, which a bare kit stands on (tested).
- **Floating is the vanity's alone.** `floatHeightOf` is null for the chest, the drawer base, the
  desk and the free panel.
- **A floating vanity is its kit hung** (`hungTypeOf`), not a second type and not a scatter of `if`
  lines. One answer feeds the legs, the toe kick, the plinth gate, the side infills'
  reach to the floor and `assemblies`.
- **The hangers are an engine order line** (the road of every bought line), not a bom.js special
  case. No drilling: no pattern of the hanger's fixing is in this repo. The spec says
  `on: 'RAIL-TOP'`.
- **Back on the floor, the plinth returns as at birth** (`takesPlinth`), not as it was before
  floating.
- **"Equal" on every `drawerColumns` kit replaces "Reset to the kit"** (B5's ask). An old chest
  saved at 4:3:2 is made equal by the same press. Other ratio kits keep their reset.
- **The Columns row is in the drawer stack (Section 1)** on every drawer-column kit, not in the
  Vanity section. It is one control for the chest and the vanity.
- **Drawers 2 / 3 / 4 swaps the kit** (`setVanityDrawers`). The row-keyed `drawer_heights` and
  `drawer_double` are cleared, because a typed height of row 3 means nothing on a new stack.
