T40-F2 — THE CNC LAYOUT FINGERPRINTS THAT MOVED, AND THE ONES THAT DID NOT

Generated: node scripts/cnc-fingerprint.mjs, on the branch before F2 and after it.
Report size: 9452 fingerprints.
Moved:       206 fingerprints.

WHAT MOVED — the SHEET fingerprint of the drawer-bank kits, and only on the
presets whose sheet actually carries a drawer box:

  kits:    BUDR, BUDR2, BUDR4, OVEN_BASE
  presets: all , non-sprayed 

WHY: F2 lays the four drawer-box boards the owner named — the sides SL and SR,
the box front BF and the box back BB — with the board's OWN HEIGHT standing up
the page. On a BUDR-style bank those boards are DRAWN `height × depth`, so the
sheet turns them 90° where it used to leave them at 0. The sheet packs from the
turned bounds, so every part after them on the page shifts with it and the whole
sheet's fingerprint moves. That is the feature, not a side effect of it.

WHAT DID NOT MOVE — not one PER-PART fingerprint, in any kit, on any preset.
A part's own CNC frame is untouched: same outline, same pockets, same holes, same
drawn size. Only the PLACEMENT changed, which is what a nesting program turns.

THE ENGINE IS A SEPARATE QUESTION and a separate file: verify/t40/byte-identity.txt
reports all six standard configs IDENTICAL with UNNAMED=0. A CNC layout fingerprint
is not an engine hash, and CLAUDE.md's iron rule 8 says to keep the two apart.
