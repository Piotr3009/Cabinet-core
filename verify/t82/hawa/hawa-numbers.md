A pocket for ONE door is 55 mm clear (160704 / Connector 55). 110 is not "one door with more room": it is 2 x (17 mm hardware + door + air) for two hardware sets, one on each pocket panel.

## 2. The pocket

### 2.1 Clear width between the two pocket panels

| | One door (overlay) | Two doors in one pocket |
|---|---|---|
| Clear width | **55 mm** | **110 mm** |
| Source | 160704 p1 front view "55"; 31418 p1 "Connector 55" | 169313 p1 front view "110"; 31418 p2 "Connector 110" step 1 "110" |

Make-up of the 55 (160704 p1 front view, 1200 dpi crop): hardware zone (vertical profile + hinge arms) **17 mm** between the inner face of the hardware panel B and the back face of the door; then the door S (18–26); the remainder (55 − 17 − S = 12–20 mm) is free air between the door front face and the other pocket panel A. Nothing is dimensioned on the panel-A side; the door is positioned only by the hardware. For 110: 17 + door + air + door + 17, mirror-symmetric (169313 p1 front view, "17" at both panels).

### 2.3 Pocket depth (front to back)

Formulas, identical in 160704 p1 and 169313 p1 tables:

- **T1 = TB + 92 + Z3 − M** (hardware depth = the depth the pocket must have from the front edge of the pocket panels to the back of the hardware). In 169313 the same formula is printed with Z3 although only Z5 is defined (see section 9).
- **T1 ≥ 414 mm** (table "T1 ≥ 414").
- **T1 ≤ 567 mm** — derived: the bottom guide profile is cut to "≤ 560 = T1 − 7" (160704 p1 and 169313 p1 "Ablängen der Profile"); 560 is the longest profile, so T1 cannot exceed 567.
- **E1 = TB − M** (slide-in depth: how far the open door goes into the pocket behind the pocket front edge).
- **M = door projection over cabinet** (the part of the open door that stays in front of the pocket front edge): **M ≥ S + 3** and **M ≤ TB − 320**. "Flush door" = M = S + 3 (open door's leading edge flush with the front face of the closed door); "protruding door" = M larger (160704 p1 "Türe bündig / Türe vorstehend" sketches).
- **KT1 ≥ T1** (160704: cabinet depth at least the hardware depth). **KT1 ≥ T1 + RE** (169313: RE undefined in the legend; in the sketch it is the zone behind T1 at the cabinet back, i.e. back-panel allowance).
- Pocket depth = door width + 92 + Z3 − M: with the flush case (M = S + 3, S = 19, Z3 = 3) the pocket depth is **TB + 73 mm** (TB 440 → 513, TB 494 → 567). If the ≤ 560 track length is a hard cap, a wider flush door is impossible and M must grow: M ≥ TB + 95 − 567 = TB − 472 (e.g. TB 500 → M ≥ 28, TB 750 → M ≥ 278; both still within M ≤ TB − 320).

Pocket height: **EH** = installation height (height of the pocket panels, 160704/169313 front views). No formula is printed; derived from the front views: EH = TH + Z1 + Z2 (door between bottom panel top face and the pocket panels' top edge). Caveat in section 9.

## 5. Push vs Pull (what differs geometrically)

- Push: push-to-open ejector unit [8] mounted on the vertical profile at mid height (visible in 160704 p1 "Hawa Concepta III 25/35 Push" side view; absent in the Pull side view); catch bracket [3] at the back of the pocket in both versions; Push cover strip [12] cut to length over the vertical profile (52679 p1 step 12); Push buffer pad on the panel with 2 holes **32** apart, nearest **14.5** from the edge (52679 p1 step 13); ejector force adjustable "up to 12 turns" (52679 p1 step 11). The door has no handle.
- Pull: door with a handle; "Pay attention to potential collision hazards when designing door handles" (169313 p1). Front clearance is the same 3 (2–4) in both versions (160704 p1 top views are common to Push/Pull).
- No other dimension differs between Push and Pull in the documents (same T1, E1, M, Z, TA, hole patterns).

## 6. The Connector (31418)

- **Connector 55** (31418 p1, "left-hand system"): closes/ties the top and bottom of the 55 mm pocket between panel A and panel B. Parts: a long profile screwed along the top edge of panel A (4.5 x 16, Torx 20), a bottom bracket (2 screws 4.5 x 16, axis 38 above the round pad), round pads at S + 43 from the front edge (60 below the top edge), a cap profile laid over the pocket top that hooks over the top track and is locked with clips (4 x 12 screws, "push the clip upwards by hand when screwing", "click"), and the same at the bottom (step 5). Thickness of the cap at the top: 6.5 (160704 p1 front view "6.5"). Length not dimensioned (cut on site).
- **Connector 110** (31418 p2): a flat bridging cap profile ("TOP") that spans the 110 pocket and hooks over BOTH hardware panels' top tracks (two cabinets, two doors in one pocket); same at the "BOTTOM" (step 3); fixed with 4 x 12 screws and Torx 20. Dimension printed: **110** (pocket clear width).
- So the connector does NOT join the top and bottom tracks of one door; it joins the two pocket panels across the pocket. "55" and "110" are the pocket clear widths they fit.

## 8. Cabinet interface

- Cabinet depth **KT1 ≥ T1** (160704) or **KT1 ≥ T1 + RE** (169313; RE not defined, = rear zone behind the hardware).
- The pocket is part of the cabinet: panel A of the pocket is the cabinet side (160704) or both pocket panels are the sides of the two adjacent cabinets (169313). Hardware depth T1 is measured from the front edges of the pocket panels (= cabinet front) backwards.
- Inset of the cabinet front behind the closed door: **3 (2–4)** between the door back face and the pocket/cabinet front edges (160704 p1 and 169313 p1 "Draufsicht"). The cabinet front and the pocket front are in one plane.
- Open door: slid in by E1 = TB − M behind the front plane; **M ≥ S + 3** still projecting in front of the cabinet (flush case M = S + 3).
- Clearance between the open door and the cabinet side (panel A): 55 − 17 − S = 12–20 mm (derived, not dimensioned).
- Cabinet top drawn ≥ 18 thick with its top face 22 below the pocket panels' top edge (160704 p1 front view "≥18", "22") — meaning of that offset not labelled.
- "Exterior cabinet connection length 560 mm": **not in the documents** as such. The only 560 is "≤ 560 = T1 − 7", the maximum length of the bottom guide track (160704 p1, 169313 p1), which caps the pocket depth at T1 = 567.
- Hinge axis: TA1/TA2 from the door edge (26–29.5); the closed door's hinge end is Z3 = 3 (2–5) inside the outer face of panel B (overlay) or Z5 = 3 (3–6) from the pocket panel (inlaid).

## 9. Unclear or conflicting numbers

1. T1 formula in 169313 uses Z3, but 169313 only defines Z5 (3–6); assume Z3 := Z5 there.
2. Z1/Z2 range is 0–6 in 160704 but 3–6 in 169313 (nominal 3 in both).
3. T1 maximum 567 is derived from "≤ 560 = T1 − 7", not stated as a limit.
4. The stacked top dimensions of 160704 (6.5, ≥10, 22, 63, ≥18) are not to scale; reading: 6.5 = Connector cap between panel tops and top panel, ≥10 top panel, 22 track screw line, 63 top hardware zone, ≥18 adjacent cabinet top. Whether Z1 is measured from the panel top edge or from the top panel underside could not be resolved.
5. "6" at the bottom track foot and "70 (≥10)" plinth: labels only, no text.
6. Side views: parts 353 (top) and 300 (bottom) at 28 from the rear edge of the pocket are not identified (possibly door guide/stop strips of the Connector set).
7. Panel B: "22" zone at the rear edge from the top to mid-height (next to the T1 − 55 bracket dimension) is not explained.
8. "RE" (169313, KT1 ≥ T1 + RE) is not in the legend.
9. Track and guide profile cross-sections, hinge arm dimensions, carriage dimensions: not dimensioned anywhere in the five PDFs (only the 17 / 63 / 50 zones).
10. Door 35 steel-profile vertical channels: drawn but not dimensioned; 100 − Z1 and 24 − Z2 could be the groove edge or axis.
11. Push buffer "32 / 14.5" (52679 step 13): on which panel/edge is not labelled in text.
12. Maximum pocket panel thickness: not given (only minimums ≥18 / ≥19).
13. The owner's "exterior cabinet connection length 560" does not appear; see section 8.
