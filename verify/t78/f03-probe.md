## THE FAULT

The L's door B hangs along Z: `engine/cabinet.js` 3871 (HEAD) writes its box
as `{ x: b.z, y: b.y, z: D - (b.x + b.w), w: b.d, h: b.h, d: b.w }`, so the
box carries the leaf's THICKNESS in `box.w` (25) and its WIDTH in `box.d`
(312.5), while the record says `w 312.5, h 770, thickness 25` and the cut
list (the BOM panel's material cell, `BomPanel.jsx` 677) says 25 at every
style, as the CSV's 313 x 770 does: the record is never wrong. The plain
leaf is a `boxGeometry(box.w, box.h, box.d)` (`UnitView.jsx` 1067, HEAD) and
is right by construction. The two CARVED builders read the box as every
other leaf's: `3d/frontDecorationSolid.js` 137 and 165 (HEAD)
`const { w, h, d: thickness } = panel.box`, so on door B the "width" is 25
and the "thickness" is 312.5 (the door's own width); the decoration outline
(312.5 x 770, the CNC frame, which `frontDecoration.js` 269 builds from
`cnc.drawn_w` / `panel.w`, correctly) is laid over that 25 wide board through
`toMesh = w / 2 - x` (168) and the solid comes out 312.5 x 770 x 312.5 (rows 19, 33, 40: own
box x -300..12.5, z -156.2..156.3), set at the box's centre by `meshOffset`
(882) with no turn: a block from x 273.5 to 586 reaching 287.5 mm back into
the carcass, its carved face on the arm's END (the +z cap at z = 898.5)
instead of the room face (+x). Grooved, Arched and Ornate A - Symmetric all
do it (the records are `ready`); Ornate A - Classic is refused on both leaves
(`ORNATE_RADIUS_FIT`, the 150 inner radius on a 340.5 / 312.5 leaf, T77 F4's
own law) and draws nothing, so it shows a plain slab. Shaker on door B:
`3d/shakerSolid.js` 92-94 (HEAD) reads the same and 97 refuses the tray
(`25 <= 2 * 60`), `panelSolids` cuts no recess for it, and the leaf is a plain
slab with no tray (rows 12, 13). Neither T76's decoration solid on the virtual parents
(the parent's leaf is decorated again by the L's own pass, 9279, on the
record, not the box), nor F4's per-arm door, nor the overlap offset of door B
(`innerTrim` 3698, 26.5 mm, taken off the WIDTH: 312.5 = 342 - 3 - 26.5)
adds to the thickness: only the SOLID is wrong, only on door B, and only when
it is carved. What the owner saw: with the doors shut the block hides in the
carcass but for its end strip (27 mm, x 558.5..585.9, where the plain leaf
shows 25, x 561..585.9; rows 22, 36, 43 against 8) and its top, coplanar with
TOP-L's top at y 770 over x 273.5..558 (row 40 against TOP-L's box in row
43), which z-fights through the carcass top; with "Open all" door B swings
the whole block out into the room, 312.5 deep (rows 23, 37, 44: z 598.5..911
against 886..911 plain, row 9; the eye gets 84 px of its top, 222 mm deep at
z 657..879, where the plain leaf shows no top at all from that camera). The
probe found no path that makes exactly 40; "okolo 40" is the owner's eye on
