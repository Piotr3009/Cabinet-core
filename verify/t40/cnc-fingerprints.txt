T40 — THE CNC FINGERPRINTS THAT MOVED, AND WHY (CLAUDE.md iron rule 8)

Report size: 9452 fingerprints.
Moved:       247.

Iron rule 8: A CNC FINGERPRINT IS NOT AN ENGINE HASH. The engine's own answer
for the six standard configs is IDENTICAL with UNNAMED=0 — verify/t40/byte-identity.txt.
Two features moved the machine's picture, both of them deliberately, and they
are separated below by the scenario that carries each.

═══ 1. F2, ONE GRAIN TRUTH — the drawer box is stood up on the SHEET ═══════

  190 fingerprints, all of them SHEET fingerprints (0 per-part: none).
  Kits: BUDR, BUDR2, BUDR4, OVEN_BASE

  The four boards the owner named as cut STANDING — the drawer sides SL and
  SR, the box front BF and the box back BB — are DRAWN `height x depth` on a
  BUDR-style bank, so the sheet turns them 90 degrees where it used to leave
  them at 0. Everything after them on the page shifts with it, so the whole
  sheet's fingerprint moves.

  THE PART'S OWN CNC FRAME IS UNTOUCHED — same outline, same pockets, same
  holes, same drawn size. Not one per-part DXF moved for this feature. Only
  the PLACEMENT changed, which is what a nesting program turns.

═══ 2. F3a, NO DOOR NO STRIP — a shoe box in a doorless cabinet ═══════════

  57 fingerprints, on the shoe-box scenarios only.
  Kits: BUDR, BUDR2, BUDR4, DW_PANEL, L_SHAPE, OVEN_BASE, WINE
  Per-part files: 01-SHOE1-BATTEN.dxf, 01-SHOE1-BF.dxf, 01-SHOE1-BK.dxf, 01-SHOE1-BT.dxf, 01-SHOE1-DV.dxf

  Every one of those kits has NO DOORS AT ALL — a drawer bank, a dishwasher
  panel, a wine rack, an L-shape, an oven base. The old rule read
  `cfg.hinge === 'L'`, which is never absent, so a shoe box inside them
  reserved a 30 mm hinge band for a door that does not exist and cut a
  30 x 70 BATTEN to carry it. Measured, on a BUDR with a drawer shoe box:

      BEFORE   SHOE1-BK 472 x 80   SHOE1-BF 472 x 80   SHOE1-BATTEN 435 x 70
      AFTER    SHOE1-BK 502 x 80   SHOE1-BF 502 x 80   (no batten)

  The boards run the full width and the batten is not cut. That is the
  owner's own sentence — "jak nie ma drzwi w ogole, to nie powinno robic
  30 mm odstepu infill na zawiasy" — reaching the shoe box through the ONE
  source (`hingedSides`) the plate drilling already reads. It is a BOM change
  as well as a CNC one: one fewer part on a shoe box in a doorless cabinet.

═══ WHAT DID NOT MOVE ═════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  Every kit and every scenario outside the two lists above is byte-identical,
  file for file and sheet for sheet — including every cabinet WITH doors,
  whose 30 mm band is cut exactly where it always was.
