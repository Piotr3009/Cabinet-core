## THE HOUSE'S HANDS, AND THREE THINGS THE PREP RUN TAUGHT

The hands are T74's and T75's (imported, never copied): `page.press` (a real
click at a control's centre), `clickWith`, `dragFromTo`, `choose` (a native
select by click, arrows, Enter), `reachFree` (a piece's own pixel the canvas
owns), `dragAlong`; the T76 `typeInto` (click, Ctrl+A, digits, Enter); the
T80 `menu`, `viewMode`, `checkOpen`; the T81 `openSet`, `sheetOnGlass`,
`frameOf` (the glass's SVG painted at 4 px per paper mm), the PDF blob read
back with `pdfinfo` and now `pdfimages`; the T77 file chooser
(`Page.setInterceptFileChooserDialog`, `DOM.setFileInputFiles`) for the logo.

1. The room is centred on the world origin (Room.jsx 189). Every scene
   reading of the walk (`sceneOf`) is turned back into PLAN millimetres: x
   along wall 1 from the room's corner, y up from the floor, z out of wall 1.
   Self-test: a BUDR at x 1000 reads x 1000, z 10 (the wall gap), y 100 (its
   legs).
2. A chimney's meshes carry `raycast={null}` (Room.jsx 315 to 361), and
   three's `intersectObjects` throws on them inside the shared pixel finder
   (`t74-harness.mjs FIND`, `t75-harness.mjs EMPTY`), which casts over the
   whole scene. The walk gives those objects a no-op before every reach
   (`safeRays`): r3f casts only at objects with handlers, so what the
   pointer meets in the app is unchanged. Said in the ledger as a NOTE.
3. On the CNC sheet a part's OUTLINE is its first `<polygon>`; a drill hole
   is a 15-point polygon too. The U of the trap is therefore read as "the
   first polygon of `[data-cnc-part=<id>]` has the engine's `cnc.outline`
   point count, more than 4", never as "some polygon has more than 4".
   And the BOM is shut by its own x (its toolbar button stands under the
   open panel and the CNC button with it), as T80's walk found.

## DECISIONS TAKEN

- The house's CDP hands (scripts/cdp.mjs, t74 and t75 harnesses), not
  Playwright, so this walk's click is the T74 to T81 walks' click; the
  brief allows either, and the house law is "one set of hands".
- A missing hook is a FAIL naming the hook and the slice; `--prep` makes it
  a WAIT and moves the ledger and the frames to a scratch folder, so a prep
  run never overwrites the evidence.
- The stages a leg is not about (a room, a chimney, the T81 kitchen) go
  through the page's store, as the T81 walk's kitchen does; every gesture a
  check is about is a real click, key or drag.
- The 750 door is asked through the BUD's own Width field (the door covers
  the BUD), read back until the door the engine cuts is 750.
- The reeds are checked by their geometry (the spacing, the centring, the
  count within one of the formula), since one cutter path per valley gives
  N - 1 or N + 1 paths for N reeds; the ledger says which.
- The PDF's logo is counted by `pdfimages -list` at the logo's own pixel
  size (96 x 64), one per page, so the Visualisation's raster cannot stand in
  for it.
- Check numbers as the brief: #45 service void (leg 2); #46 reeds, #47 HAWA
  panels 22 are not walked by F25.
