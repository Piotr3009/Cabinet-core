### `src/engine/runs.js` (A3 OWNS the pocket rows), pure

- One import: `pocketChoiceOf`, `pocketCoverOf` from `engine/hawaPocket.js`
  (A2's, read, never re-derived).
- `isPocketUnit(unit)`: the type's `hawaPocket` flag.
- `pocketNeighbours(units, pocket, profile)` -> `{ L, R }`: the units butted
  one after another outwards from the pocket on its wall (padded spans, the
  run's own `runGap`), nearest first, stopping at a unit a pocket door cannot
  close (another pocket, a free panel, a hung, turned or riding unit). It does
  NOT key on the top line, so a pocket born at the tall height still finds the
  base unit beside it and is then matched to it.
- `pocketCoverSides(units, pocket, profile)` -> `{ L: [ids], R: [ids] }`: one
  door on its hand's side, two doors on both; on a side the stored
  `params.pocket.covers` that form an unbroken chain from the pocket, else the
  ONE nearest unit (CLAUDE.md F3's default).
- `runPocketParams(units, profile, { walls })` -> `Map(id -> element | null)`,
  the twin of `runPlinthParams`: a pocket gets `{ role: 'pocket', doors, hand,
  covers, sides, beyond, cover }` (`cover` = `pocketCoverOf(...)`, the shape of
  `params.pocket_cover`; `beyond` per side: the covered unit's own side infill
  'infill', a unit butted beyond 'front' or 'endPanel', the wall within
  `doors.neighbourReachMm` 'wall', else 'none'); a covered unit gets `{ role:
  'covered', pocketId, unitNum, side }`; every other unit null.
- `levelGroups` and every existing run function are unchanged: the pocket is
  in the run because its type is a floor unit that is not a free panel.

## DECISIONS TAKEN

1. Covers resolve at READ time (the run element, T55's law): the stored
   `params.pocket.covers` is the owner's list and no settle trims it; a covered
   cabinet dragged away is uncovered and covered again when it comes back.
2. Default hand R where both or neither side has a neighbour (both HAWA
   drawings are "Anlage links": pocket left of its door).
3. The pocket's geometry is WRITTEN on every refresh from the figures (width,
   depth, wall gap) and from the covered cabinet (height, legs), never typed:
   the coordinator's contract (`params.width` / `params.depth` = `widthMm` /
   `KT`, the back stood off by `frontAlignMm`). A project-wide front change
   re-cuts the width keeping the door's edge; a growth into a neighbour is then
   the Check's UNIT_OVERLAP, said, not hidden.
4. `heightGroup: 'tall'` is A2's; the height settle follows the covered
   cabinet whatever the project's tall height says, so a pocket in a base run
   stays on the base line.
5. TB over 750 on a door of more than one unit is refused by
   `setPocketCovers`; under 440 is not refused there (adding the next is the
   remedy) and the engine's Check #37 says it.
6. `far` beyond the last covered unit: its own side infill, a butted unit
   ('front', or 'endPanel' where its facing side carries one), the wall within
   the house's neighbour reach, else 'none'; the covered unit's own end panel
   is A2's `pocketCoverOf`.
7. Two pockets never cover one cabinet: the first claims it (runPocketParams).
