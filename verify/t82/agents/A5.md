## DECISIONS TAKEN

1. The pose is engine (`src/engine/pocketDoorPose.js`), not `src/3d`. The
   plan's dashed door must be the room's open door ("the drawings are the
   model"), and the engine never imports the room.
2. The geometry is the LISP's.
   - The door's shut and open boxes are the engine's.
   - The hardware is on panel B, beside the covered cabinet. Panel A is the
     outer panel, flush with the door's face.
   - My picture only interpolates between the two boxes. Its pivot is the
     one axis that carries `closed` onto the slot of `open`; it stays clear
     of panel A at every t (tested).
3. The open state is view state: uiStore `openFronts`, keyed by the pocket
   unit and the door's id. Open all, the double click and the Door row are
   one switch. `togglePocketDoor` is the pocket's one press over one door or
   two.
4. The cups are drawn from the figures' holes on the cup layer
   (`profile.hawa.layers.cup`), as dark discs on the back (DrillRings' law),
   because the engine's `result.drills` carries none yet (A4's F5). The
   fronts-sheet bars sit at the engine's `cupY`.
5. The Push ejector's section is the profile symbol's own. Its height of 100
   is a PICTURE number in the module (`EJECTOR_PICTURE_HEIGHT_MM`, PROPOSED),
   for these reasons:
   - It is a bought part, never cut (KIT_HAWA.lsp D19).
   - A1's mirror test holds every `profile.hawa` number to a LISP constant.
   - It follows the precedent of the room's GLASS_PANE_MM.
6. The tracks appear as their zones in the room (63 / 50 x 17 x the track's
   length, D21) and as their screw axes on the section (22 and 41). There is
   no section profile, because the PDFs give none.
7. The HAWA door is drawn plain on the sheets, because the room draws it
   plain: role `pocket_door` takes no front style, per A2's decision 4.
8. A run with a pocket gets its own section station (the next letter), so
   "the section through the pocket" exists without the dropdown.
