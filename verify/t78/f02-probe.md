## THE FAULT

`worktopsFor` (src/engine/worktop.js:260) reaches `lSlabGeometry` (:434) only when the record names the L alone (`loneL = mine.length === 1 && isCornerKit(mine[0])`). The record the client writes with the selection panel's "Add worktop (n)" over the L and the cabinets along its arms (the T75 walk's own gesture; rows 8 and 11, and the app section: `BoxGeometry 1520 x 38 x 948`) falls through to `worktopGeometry` (:298, one wall) or `cornerSlabs` (:275 to :377, two walls), and both measure every unit as a rectangle through `reach(u) = depth + doors.gap + front_t + 20` (:219 to :224) and keep the deepest: the L's `depth` is its arm B (900), so the whole run's slab comes out 948 deep (342 mm too deep over a 558 Standard, row 9) with the L's inner corner stone; slab B of the corner pair is then cut to `wallB.width - a.d` = 2052, 38 mm short of the arm B end at 2090 (row 11). Even the lone L's slab (rows 4, 5) starts at `x0 = unit.position.x_mm` (:450) = 10, leaving the wall gap bare at wall B while every straight slab is flush at its wall, and its arm B end always takes the 10 side overhang (`D + side`, :457) because `endMeets` looks only along the L's own wall. The mask: `maskSegments` (src/engine/runs.js:1179) puts the L and the wall unit beside it into ONE segment and `segmentMask` (:1209) makes a rectangle `length x (max depth + 10)` of it, so the L's run element is 1200 x 610 (row 17), the WUD beside it is a `member` that cuts nothing (row 18), and cabinet.js:5663 to :5684 cuts the owner's `rectGeometry(maskLen, maskD)`: a 1200 x 610 board (600 x 610 alone, row 20) over the whole corner square, no L anywhere.

## THE FIX

Where it goes, and what landed at 21:51 UTC (f02-probe-after.md): `src/engine/worktop.js worktopsFor` routes any record holding a corner kit to a new `lRunSlabs` (after :275): the L slab `${id}-L` from `lSlabGeometry`, one straight slab `${id}-w${wall}` per wall from `worktopGeometry` plus `sideTrim`, its end set to the arm end with `butts: 'L'` and the T75 F5 butt-joint record `{ kind, boltCount, at: { wall, x: armEnd, depth }, arm: 'A' | 'B', readiness: 'manual' }`; `lSlabGeometry` (:443 onward) goes flush at wall B (`toWallB`, x 0 for the left hand, the wall's end for the right) and drops arm B's side overhang when the adjacent wall's run butts it (`cornerLAdjacentSpan`, runs.js, the F1 helper); `src/engine/runs.js maskSegments` (:1286) makes an L its own segment; `src/engine/cabinet.js` (:5677) cuts `MASK-L` from the L plan: outline `[[0,0],[610,0],[610,410],[410,410],[410,610],[0,610]]`, box from -10 at both walls, `meta.planOutline` for `3d/panelSolid.js:108`, true area 0.3321 m², the straight mask untouched (`!lCorner`).
