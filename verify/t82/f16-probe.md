## The one link the fix must change

CONFIRMED as CLAUDE.md names it, in three places. (1) The plane: `dragPlane` (src/3d/UnitView.jsx:1818-1821) passes through `D / 2` from the wall line; for a free panel it must pass through the panel's own `backStandoff` (the number the view already draws it at, src/3d/UnitView.jsx:1941). (3) The clamps: `mount_height` has none in `updateUnitParams` (row 6), and the back inset is capped at `editor.maxInset` (src/stores/projectStore.js:4820) where the room should cap a panel. The typed road gains `fp-wall` beside `fp-left` and `fp-floor` (src/3d/SpacingChain.jsx:137, src/3d/SpacingChain.jsx:150).
