## THE FAULT

`src/3d/UnitView.jsx` 1037 to 1041 places the glazed door's pane at `[-pivot[0] + mm(frame + aperture.w / 2 - box.w / 2), -pivot[1], -pivot[2]]` inside the swing group that stands at `pivot` (line 973, the hinge edge of the leaf in the UNIT frame), so in the unit frame the pane's centre is `[frame + aperture.w / 2 - box.w / 2, 0, 0]`, and that x term is 0 on every leaf because `shaker.js shakerPanelRect` (169 to 179) centres the aperture (x1 = f, x2 = w - f). The pane therefore stands at the UNIT's ORIGIN: its centre at [0, 0, 0] in the unit frame against the leaf's [300, 360, 415.5] on the 600 x 720 WUD_GLASS (rows 6 and 7), which is the bottom back left corner of the carcass, in the wall plane (world z -1492..-1488 on a wall at z -1490), half its height below the unit (world y 1200..1800 under a unit whose bottom is at 1500) and half its width past the unit's left side; the same point on the PRO wardrobe (delta [-300, -1075, -573.5], the pane's box y -915..1115 under a unit standing at 100) and on retail's wardrobe, where BOTH leaves' panes sit on that one point (deltas [-300.7, -1073.5, -580] and [-899.2, -1073.5, -580]). That is the owner's "gdzies na scianie". Because the pane IS inside the swing group, the 2klik turns it about the hinge edge from that wrong place: the leaf's centre moves [-345.2, 0, 294.8] and the pane's [412.1, 0, 479] (wall unit), [568.2, 0, 661.7] (wardrobe), into the carcass (frames 04, 08, 12). It is one frame error, in the view only: the engine's `meta.glass` (cabinet.js 7772 to 7778) carries frame and aperture, which is enough because the aperture is centred; the BOM line is right; the mirror sixteen lines above (1017 to 1024) backs the leaf's own centre `mm(p.box.x + p.box.w / 2)` out of the pivot and is right. PRO and retail draw the room with the same UnitView.jsx, so both show it; a tall leaf (2150) and a wall leaf (720) show it the same way.

## THE FIX

`src/3d/UnitView.jsx` 1038 to 1042 (one writer): the pane takes the mirror's own formula, z at the leaf's mid thickness: `[mm(p.box.x + p.box.w / 2) - pivot[0], mm(p.box.y + p.box.h / 2) - pivot[1], mm(p.box.z + p.box.d / 2) - pivot[2]]`. It is absolute minus pivot, so it holds for every pivot kind the file has (hinge L and R, the drop front about its bottom edge, the L's door B hung along Z) and the swing follows with no second animation, shut and open, PRO and retail: evaluated on the probe's own records the patched position in the swing group is [298.5, 0, 0] on WG01-F, exactly the door mesh's own, and [-297.7, 0, 0] on retail's hinge R leaf, again the door mesh's (last row). Patch written: `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-Cabinet-core/cd589afd-2c93-5713-8e6d-8d2ee0c863fd/scratchpad/patches/f12-unitview-pane.patch` (one hunk, `git apply --check` passes on the working tree; note `f12-unitview-pane.md` beside it). No engine, store or profile line moves and no golden can reach a view file. Proof for `test/turn78-f12-glass.test.js`: the probe's page-side MEASURE (pane centre in the unit frame equals `p.box` centre within 0.1 mm, shut AND open; the pane's world displacement under a 2klik equals the door mesh's), and the walk re-takes frames 02 and 04. The second half (smoke by default, with a choice) goes: `profile.js` 120 (`front.glass = { kinds: ['clear', 'smoke', 'reeded'], default: 'smoke' }` beside `front.types.GL`), `cabinet.js` 7772 (`meta.glass.kind` from the door's param or the default) and 9741 to 9748 (the merge key and the label take the kind: "Smoked glass 4 mm, 477 x 600 mm aperture"; the 4 is `GLASS_PANE_MM`, UnitView.jsx 14, a picture number the owner confirms before it is written on an order), `UnitView.jsx` 45 to 55 (a table keyed by kind in place of the one `PANE_ALPHA_GLASS`: clear lighter and thinner, smoke today's #5a4636 at 0.28, reeded today's plus a vertical ridge normal map generated in code), the choice in PRO's Doors section (RightPanel.jsx, beside the Hinge side select) and retail's FRONTS step (Options.jsx 735 to 790, under the style list, for GL only).
