## F5 · CNC: THE PANELS' HOLES AND THE DOOR'S CUPS

What it does: every LISP hole becomes a drill record, seated in the house's CNC frame for its board:

| Board | Frame | x | y |
| - | - | - | - |
| panel on the pocket's left (A at hand R, B at hand L, B-L) | BUL's (its pocket face, the front at x 0) | u | v |
| panel on the pocket's right (B at hand R, A at hand L, B-R) | BUR's (the front at x = w; D20 "mirrored") | w - u | v |
| door, cups on its left edge seen from the room (hingeEdge L) | a FRONT's (seen from its back) | w - u | v |
| door, cups on its right edge | a FRONT's | u | v |

## DECISIONS TAKEN

1. The order lines are engine hardware lines built in `cnc/hawa.js` and pushed with literal roles (the
   registry's scan law), as Cabineo's are: one path that the cut list, the purchase list and the invoice read.
2. The article prints inside spec_label (` · art. N`), as the Conero pull-down rail line does; no bomInvoice
   change. An empty article leaves the name alone.
3. The HAWA hinges are their own role (`hawa_hinges`), never `hinges`: the house's role buys a plate per hinge.
4. A door with no class orders nothing of its own; the connector and the foot stay with the pocket.
5. The steel profile: one per routed groove (A1's two grooves a III/35 door).
6. Frames: each board as seen from its drilled face, the house's rule (BUL / BUR / FRONT) and the PDFs' own
   panel drawings (160704 "Beschlagseite B" with the front at the right). KIT_HAWA.lsp's command `c:HAWA_FULL`
   draws every panel unmirrored at x0 + u; its D20 says B-L and B-R are mirrored. The face coordinates (u, v)
   are the LISP's to the byte; only the seat in the board's frame is the house's. For A1 to align the drawing
   command if the owner wants the LISP's own DXF to match.
7. The spray law: the door rule 1 (it is a door), the panels rule 7 (PROPOSED: one face, the back framed, four
   edges). A rule of their own would be a 21st row of the owner's twenty (test/turn78-f21-spray.test.js).
8. The scene placements (joinery.js) are added in F5 so the room's and the sheets' bores read the record (R10).
