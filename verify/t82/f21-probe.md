## The one link the fix must change

CONFIRMED as CLAUDE.md names it, with one correction of WHERE. The drop is `heightChains` (src/engine/drawings/wallElevation.js:268-269, the T41 F5c block from 250): the edge closer than `floorMm` (the door gap, 3) to the last one is dropped and the gap falls into the next front. But the T81 fronts sheet's chain is drawn by `runChains` in src/engine/drawings/setElevation.js:168-169 (no slice's file: INT's), not by `wallElevation.js verticalChains` (src/engine/drawings/wallElevation.js:342, the T40 sheet's). So FRONT, GAP, FRONT is the drop removed in `heightChains` (E2's file) and read as is by `runChains`; the figure that does not fit is already lifted onto a leader by `chainV` (src/engine/drawings/setChains.js:94-123), so nothing new is needed for it.
